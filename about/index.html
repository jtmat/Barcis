About Barcis

Barcis is a personal collection of ideas, tools, and lessons worth keeping.

It brings together practical thoughts on money, travel, technology, health, and the small systems that can make life simpler. It is not meant to provide the final word on every subject. Often, the most useful thing I can do is explain what has worked for me and point toward people who have covered the subject better.

The site is intentionally quiet: useful writing, carefully chosen links, and templates that solve a real problem.
